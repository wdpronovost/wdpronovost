WHAT COMES BACK, REVIEW AFTER REVIEW

Four things managers keep writing down.

01

Steps up before being asked

When the department lost its leadership, I took on the coordination, the executive updates, and the incident response without waiting for a title to make it official. Reviews across two managers describe the same pattern.

Recurring theme · 2022, 2025, 2026 reviews

02

Translates technical work for non-technical people

The consistent note is that I can take a system-level problem and make it legible to finance, planning, or an executive team — and that this is what makes the technical work land.

Recurring theme · 2025, 2026 reviews

03

The person others route work through

Both managers used the same phrase independently: the go-to resource. Peers cited mentorship and knowledge-sharing without being asked to.

Recurring theme · 2025, 2026 reviews

04

Ships, and ships early

Where there are dates, the work landed ahead of them — a support portal five months early, a goal of three enhancements delivered as six. Where a goal failed, it is because it was never started, and I said so in advance.

Recurring theme · 2024, 2025 reviews

Drawn from six years of written performance reviews by two managers. Full references available on request.
